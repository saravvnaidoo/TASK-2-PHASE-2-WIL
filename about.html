A local idea. A bigger arena.

Jason Naidoo started Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena in 2023 to give local players access to high-spec PCs, current-gen consoles and quality peripherals without needing to own any of the hardware themselves.

Whether you are practising with a team or meeting friends for a few games, the setup is ready when you arrive.

OUR MISSION

Make better play accessible.

Give every player a welcoming place to play on quality equipment, whatever their home setup looks like.

OUR VISION

Grow the local game.

Build a connected gaming community where casual players, teams and event organisers can level up together.
